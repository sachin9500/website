Innovation at core

FasciCore Labs is a deep-tech startup building a full-stack AI acceleration platform to make AI inference more efficient and cost-effective for data centers.

Our platform is designed around workload-aware optimization, improving how AI workloads use compute, memory, and power across the stack. By grounding our approach in real-world inference behavior, we reduce inefficiencies, lower total cost of ownership, and enable scalable AI deployment under practical constraints such as power, thermal limits, and infrastructure flexibility.

Starting with enterprise and private data centers in India, FasciCore Labs is building an AI acceleration platform that evolves from early efficiency validation into purpose-built acceleration, supporting long-term performance, scalability, and deployment reliability.
