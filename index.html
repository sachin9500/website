02 / Our approach

Optimize beyond the model.

FasciCore is exploring a full-stack approach to AI inference acceleration. The goal is to connect workload behavior with system-level decisions, rather than optimize each layer in isolation.

We are early in the journey: validating the technical approach, identifying high-value workloads, and learning alongside potential infrastructure partners.
